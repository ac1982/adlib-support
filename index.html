Read your script, record your video, and review your take in one place. Adlib is a free teleprompter for iPhone and iPad.

Get started

  1. Choose “Paste or type a script” or try the built-in sample.
  2. Prepare your script and adjust its text size and reading speed.
  3. Choose “Record video” and tap “Start recording”. Allow camera and microphone access when asked.
  4. Finish your take, review it, and allow Photos access to save it.

For rehearsal without a camera, choose “Prompter only”. You can also connect a folder in Files to read Markdown scripts.

Need help?

Report a problem or ask a question. Include your device model, iOS version, Adlib version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Please do not post private scripts, personal videos, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Permissions and troubleshooting

Camera and microphone permissions are needed for recording. Photos permission lets Adlib add finished videos to your library. Speech recognition is optional and used for on-device voice-following. You can change permissions in Settings → Apps → Adlib.

If saving fails, keep the app open and check available storage and Photos permission. Adlib can offer recovery for interrupted recordings. Back up important scripts and recordings before removing the app.
