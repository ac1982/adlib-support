Adlib Support

Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Adlib is provided by Pulse (Chongqing) Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. Adlib does not require an account, include advertising or third-party analytics, or collect your scripts, recordings, speech, or usage data on our servers.

Your scripts and preferences

Scripts you create are stored on your device. You can grant access to a folder through Apple's Files picker. If you choose an iCloud Drive or another cloud-provider folder, that provider handles synchronization under its own privacy policy. Some app preferences may synchronize through your Apple iCloud account. We do not have access to your iCloud content.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

Adlib uses the camera and microphone when you choose to record. Recordings are processed on your device and saved to Photos with your permission. Temporary or recoverable recordings can remain in the app's local storage. Adlib requests add-only access to Photos to save videos; it does not browse your photo library. Your device's Photos and backup settings may synchronize saved content with Apple.

Voice following

Optional voice following uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Adlib requires on-device recognition for this feature and does not send speech to our servers. Availability depends on the device and language.

Your control

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete scripts through the app or Files and delete saved videos through Photos. Removing the app removes its local app data, but does not remove copies in Photos, external folders, cloud storage, or device backups.

Support and this website

If you contact us or submit a support issue, we receive the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. GitHub hosts this support website and issue tracker and processes visits and submissions under its own privacy policy. Public issues are visible to others; do not include confidential information.

Changes and questions

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. For questions, contact us through the support tracker.
